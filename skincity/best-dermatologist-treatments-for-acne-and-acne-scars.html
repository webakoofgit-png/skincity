Best Dermatologist Treatments for Acne and Acne Scars

Updated on July 20, 2025 • By Skincity Official

Acne is a common skin concern affecting people of all ages. While active acne can be frustrating, the scars it leaves behind can impact confidence. Fortunately, modern dermatology offers effective solutions for both acne and acne scars.

Treatments for Active Acne

1. Chemical Peels

These treatments remove dead skin cells and unclog pores.

Result: Reduced acne and smoother skin.

2. Laser Therapy

Laser targets acne-causing bacteria and reduces inflammation.

Result: Long-term acne control.

3. Oral Medications

Dermatologists may prescribe antibiotics or isotretinoin for severe acne.

Result: Treats acne from within.

4. Topical Treatments

Creams with retinoids, benzoyl peroxide, or salicylic acid help control oil and bacteria.

Result: Prevents new breakouts.

Treatments for Acne Scars

1. Microneedling

Stimulates collagen production using fine needles.

Benefit: Improves skin texture and reduces scars.

2. Laser Resurfacing

Removes damaged skin layers and promotes new skin growth.

Benefit: Effective for deep scars.

3. PRP Therapy (Vampire Facial)

Uses platelet-rich plasma from your blood to heal skin.

Benefit: Natural rejuvenation.

4. Dermal Fillers

Fillers are used to lift depressed scars.

Benefit: Instant visible improvement.

5. Subcision

A minor procedure to break scar tissue under the skin.

Benefit: Ideal for deep acne scars.

Final Note

Clear skin is not just about appearance—it boosts confidence. With the right treatments and expert guidance, you can effectively manage acne and reduce scars for healthier skin.
