Vitiligo Treatment in India: Latest Medical and Laser Solutions

Updated on September 1, 2025 • By Skincity Official

Vitiligo is a skin condition that causes loss of pigmentation, leading to white patches on different parts of the body. While it is not harmful or contagious, it can impact confidence and emotional well-being. With advancements in dermatology, India now offers some of the most effective medical and laser treatments for vitiligo.

What is Vitiligo?

Vitiligo occurs when melanocytes (pigment-producing cells) are destroyed or stop functioning. The exact cause is unknown, but it is often linked to autoimmune disorders, genetics, or stress.

Common Symptoms

White patches on skin (face, hands, legs, lips).

Premature whitening of hair.

Patchy loss of skin color spreading over time.

Latest Medical Treatments for Vitiligo

Topical Medications

Dermatologists prescribe corticosteroids or calcineurin inhibitors.

Benefit: Helps restore pigmentation in early stages.

Oral Medications

Medicines may be given to control autoimmune response.

Benefit: Slows down progression of vitiligo.

Phototherapy (NB-UVB Therapy)

Narrowband UVB light therapy is one of the most effective treatments.

Benefit: Stimulates melanocyte activity and repigmentation.

Excimer Laser Therapy

A targeted laser treatment used for small and stable patches.

Benefit: Faster and more precise results compared to traditional therapy.

Advanced Laser and Surgical Solutions

Laser Treatment (Excimer Laser)

This advanced laser emits UVB light directly on affected areas.

Results: Visible repigmentation within a few sessions for suitable cases.

Skin Grafting (Surgical Option)

Healthy pigmented skin is transplanted to affected areas.

Best for: Stable vitiligo cases.

Melanocyte Transplantation

Pigment cells are extracted, cultured, and applied to depigmented areas.

Benefit: Long-lasting and natural-looking results.

Why Choose India for Vitiligo Treatment?

Access to advanced dermatology clinics and technologies.

Experienced dermatologists at affordable costs.

Availability of both medical and surgical solutions under one roof.

Important Care Tips

Apply sunscreen regularly to protect affected areas.

Avoid skin injuries (cuts, burns).

Maintain a healthy diet and manage stress.

Follow dermatologist-recommended treatment consistently.
