Top 5 Reasons Why You Might Be Losing Hair and How to Fix It

Introduction
Hair loss is a concern shared by millions of people worldwide. Whether it’s a gradual thinning of hair or sudden shedding, losing hair can take a toll on your confidence and self-image. At Skincity India, we believe that identifying the cause is the first step toward effective treatment. Here are the top 5 reasons why you might be losing hair—and what you can do to fix it.

1. Genetics and Hereditary Hair Loss (Androgenetic Alopecia)
One of the most common causes of hair loss in both men and women is hereditary hair thinning, also known as androgenetic alopecia. This type of hair loss is gradual and usually follows a predictable pattern—receding hairline or thinning crown for men, and diffuse thinning in women.

2. Hormonal Imbalance
Hormones play a major role in hair health. Conditions such as PCOS, thyroid disorders, pregnancy, or menopause can lead to temporary or long-term hair loss in women.
Solution:
Addressing the underlying hormonal imbalance through medical treatment is essential. Hormone-stabilizing therapies, combined with hair rejuvenation procedures like Mesotherapy or PRP, can significantly improve hair density.
3. Poor Nutrition and Crash Dieting
Your hair needs a variety of nutrients to grow and remain strong. Deficiencies in iron, protein, Vitamin D, B12, or zinc can lead to hair thinning, breakage, and shedding.
Solution:
Ensure your diet includes essential nutrients or consider supplementation under medical guidance. At Skincity India, we offer diet consultations along with treatments to support hair regeneration.
4. Stress and Lifestyle Habits
Chronic stress, irregular sleep, smoking, and alcohol consumption can impact your scalp health and disrupt the hair growth cycle, leading to telogen effluvium (a form of temporary hair loss).
Solution:
Stress management, lifestyle adjustments, and scalp stimulation therapies can help reverse the condition. Our holistic treatment approach combines wellness with dermatological care.
5. Scalp Conditions and Dandruff
Untreated scalp infections, seborrheic dermatitis, and excessive dandruff can weaken hair roots and cause shedding.
Solution:
Treating the scalp is just as important as treating the hair. Medicated shampoos, anti-fungal treatments, and scalp exfoliation therapies can clean the environment for healthy regrowth.
