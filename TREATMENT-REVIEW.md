## Material corrections

- Removed blanket claims of guaranteed safety, painless procedures, scar-free results, universal suitability and fixed or complete improvement.
- Corrected the claim that Lipodissolve is US FDA approved. FDA status is not a determination of Indian authorisation; do not infer one from the other.
- Distinguished surgical/cannula-based procedures from non-invasive body contouring; removed the suggestion that anaesthetic choice alone guarantees liposuction safety.
- Clarified that FUE can leave small scars, tattoo clearance depends on ink and skin, and laser treatment cannot erase all scars or stretch marks.
- Separated microwave underarm treatment from laser sweat ablation, replacing the duplicated description. Removed the unsupported 70–80% success figure.
- Updated laser hair growth benefits to conditional hair-density improvement and practical features, with repeated treatment and variable response explained.
- Removed routine claims for skin-lightening infusions in dark-circle care and added the need to identify the underlying cause.
- Replaced assured vaginal laser outcomes with assessment-led information and the limitations of evidence for the advertised uses.
- Qualified nail-laser claims rather than presenting laser as an established cure for fungal infection.
- Removed unrelated keloid text from the edited content of other treatment pages.

## Clinic confirmation required

1. **Growth Factor / Triple Growth Factor:** exact preparation, ingredients, source, meaning of “triple”, delivery route, local authorisation, diagnosis-specific studies, risks and follow-up. The copy does not invent a protocol or claim verified regrowth benefits.
2. **Mesotherapy:** preparation, doses, delivery method, evidence and consent information. The existing `pain-free-mesotherapy` URL remains, but the visible title is Scalp Mesotherapy to avoid promising absence of pain.
3. **Bio-FUE:** what the clinic adds to standard FUE, surgeon qualifications and procedure-specific outcomes. Do not use “no scar” as a guarantee.
4. **Vitiligo protocols:** components of “steroid free” and “external stabilisation”, and evidence for stopping spread. Excimer/grafting options require individual clinical selection.
5. **Devices and injectables:** actual models, indications, product labels and applicable Indian authorisations. No blanket FDA claims should be restored without device/product-specific evidence.
6. **Images:** laser hair reduction currently uses an existing clinic-team photo, not an unrelated scalp laser or acne procedure. Replace it with an approved, correctly labelled hair-reduction photo when available. Nutritional and Growth Factor pages retain the existing general scalp-assessment photo.
7. **Vaginal health:** appropriate specialist review, exact proposed indication, alternatives and consent documentation before advertising a specific procedure or benefit.
8. **Clinic-specific claims:** package inclusions/prices, session schedules, named affiliations, historic “first in India” claims and success rates need clinic documentation. They have not been treated as independently verified facts.

## Scope limits

Microscopic Hair Analysis now describes clinical trichoscopy only, using the DermNet trichoscopy and AAD hair-loss assessment references above. Forensic comparisons, animal/human identification, ancestry, medullary indices and toxicology timelines are excluded as unrelated to the advertised clinic assessment. Magnified scalp images are distinguished from laboratory hair testing and are not presented as tests for nutrient deficiencies, hormones or toxins. Brochure-listed hair procedures link to their existing detail pages.

Scalp Mesotherapy copy was adapted from supplied material and checked against [the supplied systematic review](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11152360/) and the DermNet reference above. Unconfirmed drug mixtures, brands, session schedules, fixed results timelines and claims that post-treatment shedding is necessarily normal are omitted. The page advises against stopping prescribed blood thinners without the prescribing clinician's advice. Related brochure-listed treatments link to their existing pages; no duplicate Mesotherapy page was created.

The Hair Transplantation page was shortened around brochure-listed Bio-FUE, with links to Mesotherapy, Growth Factor Therapy, Triple Growth Factor Therapy and Laser Hair Growth Therapy. FUT is not advertised as a clinic service because its availability has not been confirmed. Fixed recovery times, invisible-scar claims and guaranteed lifetime growth are not used. General surgical information was checked against the NHS hair-transplant reference above.

The Chronic Skin Disease page now lists six condition names as links, without inline descriptions. Vitiligo links to its existing page. Five concise detail pages were added at `/eczema`, `/psoriasis`, `/rosacea`, `/seborrhoeic-dermatitis` and `/hidradenitis-suppurativa`, with image-first layout and a link back. These currently use the existing clinic-team photo pending approved condition-specific images. Their medical references are linked on each page. They are also linked in the treatment directory and sitemap.

This review covers the treatment detail routes above, their new directory and homepage discovery links. It does not rewrite the site's historical blog archive or certify the clinic's services, equipment, qualifications or outcomes. Publication remains a separate action.
